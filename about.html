LABUAN BAJO

Labuan Bajo is the adventurous gateway to Komodo National Park, where ancient dragons roam wild islands and sunsets paint the sea in gold. Once a quiet fishing village on Flores, it’s now a buzzing hub for divers, explorers, and dreamers chasing pink beaches, hidden caves, and some of the best underwater worlds on Earth. From sailing between jagged karst islands to diving with manta rays and trekking to panoramic viewpoints, Labuan Bajo blends raw nature with a laid-back island vibe that makes every visit feel like a real-life adventure.
