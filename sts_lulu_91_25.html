WONDERFUL
Labuan Bajo

Welcome to Labuan Bajo! Labuan Bajo is a beautiful port town on the west side of Flores Island, Indonesia. It is known as the gateway to Komodo National Park, home of the famous Komodo dragons.

Once a quiet fishing village, Labuan Bajo is now one of Indonesia’s top travel destinations. On this website, you can explore its amazing nature, islands, and adventures.
